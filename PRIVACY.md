# Privacy Policy for StreamStats

**Last Updated: November 10, 2026**

StreamStats – Video Resolution is a Safari browser extension for macOS that allows users to view the current playback resolution of videos on Netflix.

## 1. Data Collection

StreamStats does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party data collection services.

## 2. How StreamStats Works

StreamStats reads the video dimensions reported by Safari's HTML video element to determine the current playback resolution.

This information is processed locally on the user's device and displayed in the extension's popup.

StreamStats does not access, download, record, or store video content.

## 3. Website Access

StreamStats requires permission to interact with Netflix webpages to detect video playback resolution.

This permission is used solely to provide the extension's functionality.

StreamStats does not collect browsing history or transmit website activity to the developer.

## 4. Third-Party Services

StreamStats is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Netflix.

Netflix's own privacy practices are governed by its separate privacy policy.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if StreamStats' functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
